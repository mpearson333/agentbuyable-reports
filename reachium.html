How We Test

Methodology

Every platform above is queried in a neutral, non-personalized search state, the same blank-slate encounter a stranger who has never heard of this business would get, with no saved location, browsing history, or account history involved. Testing it yourself in your own browser can return a different, more favorable answer, since personal search history, location, and account context can help a platform resolve an ambiguous or similarly-named business correctly. This audit measures what AI does without that help, since that is what most new customers researching a business for the first time will actually experience. A platform defaulting to a different, similarly-named business in this neutral state points to a real entity authority gap, not a personalization gap.
